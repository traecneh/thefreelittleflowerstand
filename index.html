How to Donate

At The Free Little Flower Stand, every flower, vase, and jar has a story, and we invite you to be a part of ours. Your donations of glass jars, vases, and flowers help us keep the stand stocked and continue spreading joy in our community, one bouquet at a time.

  1. Message us on Facebook. Let us know what you’d like to donate.
  2. Coordinate a drop-off. We’ll reply with the easiest way to get it to us.
  3. Donate and brighten someone’s day. We’ll put your items to work in the stand as soon as we can.

Thank you for your kindness and for being a part of our journey to make the world a little brighter.

About

The Free Little Flower Stand is a small, family-run project built on a simple idea: free flowers for anyone who needs a smile.

This whole adventure started when Alyssa, who once doubted her flower-arranging and selling skills, realized that giving them away for free made everything bloom even brighter. With Barry's full support (and muscle power for carrying those heavy vases), we've turned this into a joy-spreading little routine.

Our toddler is the chief flower inspector, always ready to lend a hand (or two tiny ones). Our baby might not be able to handle the shears just yet, but he’s already mastered the art of posing for the camera.

We believe a simple act of kindness can make the world a little brighter. Whether you're receiving a bouquet, seeing the photos online, or just smiling as you drive by, we hope it adds a bit of light to your day.
