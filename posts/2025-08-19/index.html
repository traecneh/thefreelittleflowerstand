Bouquets, Not Arrangements!

This week we’re doing something a little different — bouquets instead of arrangements!

Being the novice, baby flower farmer that I am, I JUST learned the difference between an arrangement and a bouquet. Turns out, I’ve been making arrangements this whole time! 😅

The garden is shifting seasons — some of our early spring favorites are saying goodbye, while new late-summer beauties are stepping in. Today’s bouquets are leaning into those end-of-summer, early fall vibes with:

Celosia

Amaranth

Zinnia

Floss Flower (Ageratum)

Salvia

And of course, plenty of cheerful Black-Eyed Susans to brighten things up, plus some whimsical cosmos to keep it playful.

Stop by and grab a bouquet to bring a little sunshine home!

- The Free Little Flower Stand
