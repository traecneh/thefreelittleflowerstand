Morning Encore!

We set the flowers out later than usual last night, so only a few found homes. Consider this a continuation of that share—a proper daytime encore.

The vases you see today are the same ones we made with the help of a wonderful best friend, each put together with care and a hopeful heart.

Gardens remind us that timing isn’t always perfect—and that’s okay. If something doesn’t bloom one day, try again the next.

Light shifts, buds open, and new chances arrive with the morning. If a vase makes you smile, please take it for your table or for someone who could use a lift.

Jar/vase returns or donations are always welcome when it’s easy for you. Here, the flowers are always free—and so is the do‑over.

- The Free Little Flower Stand
