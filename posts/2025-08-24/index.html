Friendship in Full Bloom

Today’s stand comes with a little extra heart.

A wonderful woman—our best friend—joined us at the table, and together we tucked stems, and trimmed edges until each vase felt like a small thank‑you to the world.

We’re keeping today’s ingredients a surprise; just trust that the garden had plenty to say.

If one makes you smile, please take it. If you have a jar or vase to return or donate later, we’d be grateful.

Here, the flowers are always free—and the joy is meant to be shared.

- The Free Little Flower Stand
