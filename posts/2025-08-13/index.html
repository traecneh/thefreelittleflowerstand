Weathered, but Wonderful

This week’s flowers have been through a bit more life than usual, a stretch of really hot days, some serious pruning, and I didn’t get to arranging them as quickly as I’d hoped.

But they still have so much beauty to share, and I didn’t want them to go to waste.

So, I gathered them up and made some cheerful arrangements for anyone who’d like to take one home and enjoy.
