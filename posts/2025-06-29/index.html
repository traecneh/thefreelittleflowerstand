June 29, 2025 – Season’s First Bloom

The sun finally won its tug-of-war with spring showers, and our garden answered with a joyful chorus of color. This morning, The Free Little Flower Stand is alive again—our first bouquets of the year are waiting on the table, ready to wander off with anyone who needs a smile.

Today’s arrangements celebrate summer’s early overachievers: airy Cosmos dancing on the breeze, cheerful Black-Eyed Susans (Alyssa’s forever favorite), golden Rudbeckia standing tall beside soft clusters of Yarrow. We tucked in fragrant sprigs of Basil, Sage, and Mint so every bundle travels with its own little cloud of kitchen-garden goodness. And of course there are a few surprise stems sprinkled in—because gardens, like life, love a good plot twist.

If last season’s jars are still hanging out on your porch, they’d love a reunion—just leave them on the stand or swap them for a fresh bunch. And if you have extra vases looking for work, we’ll gladly give them a new purpose.

Here’s to new beginnings, sun-warmed petals, and the simple magic of giving beauty away. Thanks for stopping, sharing, and blooming right along with us.

— With love, The Free Little Flower Stand
