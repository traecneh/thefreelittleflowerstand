July 15, 2025 – Fresh‑Picked Joy Drop!

Today’s bouquets are a riot of summer cheer, starring: Queen Anne’s lace, Black‑Eyed Susans, cosmos, yarrow, zinnias, snapdragons, salvia, dill, basil, sage, oregano, bachelor’s buttons, and splashy coleus leaves for extra wow.

Life throws weeds, but gardens teach us: roots push past rock, stems stretch toward light, and every bloom opens on its own time. If you’re facing your own tough patch, let these flowers be a small reminder that growth happens—even when it’s hard to see at first. Keep reaching for the sun; brighter days are already on the way.

Swing by The Free Little Flower Stand, choose a bundle, and share a smile (or three) along the road. Take one for yourself, or one for a friend. Kindness, like petals in the breeze, travels farther than we imagine.

Here’s to new beginnings, sun-warmed petals, and the simple magic of giving beauty away. Thanks for stopping, sharing, and blooming right along with us.

— With gratitude and handfuls of happy color, The Free Little Flower Stand
